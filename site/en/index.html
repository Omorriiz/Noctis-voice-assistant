Privacy

Private by default

Noctis is a voice speaker with AI: timers, alarms, music and control from your personal account. Speech is processed by its own models, with no third-party clouds.

  • No third-party clouds — Speech recognition and replies are computed by Noctis’s own models. Audio never goes to outside AI services.
  • Nothing extra — Voice recordings are not stored. Conversation history is optional and can be erased.
  • Your data — Your music stays yours; external sources are only Jamendo and the radio you choose.

Features

Features

Everything a home speaker should do — and nothing extra.

01

Voice conversation

Wake word “Nils”, then free conversation. Understands Russian, English, Spanish, German and French.

02

Timers and alarms

They run on the speaker itself, even when the internet is down. Weekday repeats, four melodies, stop with a button.

03

Music

Your own library, the free Jamendo catalogue and internet radio. “Nils, play some jazz” — and it plays.

04

Character and voice

Ten characters from friendly to sarcastic, a male or female voice, and a choice of wake word: “Nils” or “Alexa”.

05

Privacy

Speech is processed by Noctis’s own models, not third-party clouds. Conversation history can be turned off and erased.

06

Browser dashboard

Settings, timers, alarms, music and history from any device. A mobile app is on the way.

How it works

How it works

01

Connect

The speaker gets its Wi‑Fi over Bluetooth and is linked to your account.

02

Say “Nils”

The speaker detects the wake word by itself and only then sends your phrase to the server.

03

Control

Change the voice, volume and music by voice or in the dashboard.

Microphone→Speaker (ESP32-S3)→Noctis server: speech → AI → voice→Loudspeaker

Commands

What you can say

Commands are understood in your own words, not from a fixed template.

“Nils, what time is it?”time
“Nils, set a timer for five minutes”timer
“Nils, wake me at 7:30 on weekdays”alarm
“Nils, play jazz”music
“Nils, volume to forty”volume
“Nils, tell me a bedtime story”AI conversation
